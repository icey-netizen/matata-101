Maasai Mara, Kenya

Kenya's Premier Safari Destination

Discover the Magic

Maasai Mara is Kenya's iconic safari destination, famous for the Great Migration, vast savannahs, and unforgettable wildlife encounters. From sunrise game drives to cultural visits with Maasai communities, this reserve offers a perfect blend of adventure and authentic Kenyan heritage.

Must-Visit Attractions

Experience the best of what Maasai Mara has to offer

Great Migration Plains

Witness vast savannah landscapes and seasonal wildebeest migration crossings.

Mara River Crossings

Watch dramatic river crossings and spot crocodiles, hippos, and big cats nearby.

Ol Kinyei Conservancy

Explore scenic conservancy landscapes with rich wildlife viewing and guided bush walks.

Mara Camp Experience

Stay near the reserve in safari camps offering cultural evenings and sunset viewpoints.

Maasai Cultural Boma

Experience traditional songs, dances, storytelling, and handcrafted artifacts with local hosts.

Nea Kameni Volcano

Take a boat trip to the active volcano crater and natural hot springs in the caldera.

Essential Travel Information

Everything you need to know for your Maasai Mara adventure

Best Time to Visit

April to November for warm weather. Peak season: June-August. Shoulder seasons offer fewer crowds and lower prices.

Weather & Climate

Mild highland-safari climate with warm days and cooler evenings. Light layers are ideal for early morning and sunset game drives.

Currency & Language

Kenyan Shilling (KSh) is the local currency. English and Swahili are widely spoken, with Maa commonly spoken in nearby communities.

Visa Requirements

EU citizens need only an ID card. US, Canada, Australia citizens need valid passport for stays up to 90 days.

Local Tips & Cultural Insights

  • Book sunset dinner reservations well in advance
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes for cobblestone streets
  • Carry sun protection - equatorial sun can be intense
  • Respect local customs and dress codes at religious sites
  • Try local specialties: fava beans, cherry tomatoes, and Assyrtiko wine
  • Use public transportation or rent ATVs for island exploration
